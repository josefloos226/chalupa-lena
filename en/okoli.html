EXPLORE

Nature on your doorstep.

Two landscapes. A new direction every day.

Bohemian Switzerland and the Lusatian Mountains.
On foot, by bike, with children or in winter.

EXPLORE BY SEASON

Every season has its own charm.

We've chosen a few places we'd recommend to LENA guests. This isn't a complete guide to the area, just a good starting point for a day outdoors.

From Dolní Chřibská, you can explore both Bohemian Switzerland and the Lusatian Mountains. Head towards Jetřichovice and Dolský mlýn for sandstone scenery or discover the forests and lookout towers around Jedlová and Studenec. Choose an outing to suit the season and the weather. LUSATIAN MOUNTAINS

By car, by bike or on foot. For views and history.

Places in the Lusatian Mountains worth visiting in every season.

WALKING & CYCLING · ALL YEAR

Jedlová hill

776 m above sea level · observation tower

One of the most distinctive peaks in the Lusatian Mountains. Its historic observation tower offers far-reaching views of the surrounding landscape. A great destination for a walk or cycle ride.

13.3 kmapprox. 21 min by car
Find out more →
WALKING & CYCLING · ALL YEAR

Luž

792 m above sea level · observation tower

The highest peak in the Lusatian Mountains and a beautiful destination all year round. A modern observation tower offers far-reaching views over the Czech and German countryside.

24.4 kmapprox. 32 min by car
Find out more →
WALKING & CYCLING · ALL YEAR

Křížová hora

577 m above sea level · Stations of the Cross

A historic Stations of the Cross trail above Jiřetín pod Jedlovou, with a chapel and eleven stations. An atmospheric short walk with views, easily combined with a visit to Jiřetín or a longer walk towards Jedlová.

8.8 kmapprox. 11 min by car
Find out more →
WALKING & CYCLING · ALL YEAR

Tolštejn Castle

670 m above sea level · castle ruins

The ruins of a medieval castle tucked away in the Lusatian Mountains. Tolštejn combines history, a rocky summit and beautiful views of the surrounding countryside.

10.8 kmapprox. 17 min by car
Find out more →
WALKING & CYCLING · ALL YEAR

Studenec observation tower

736 m above sea level · observation tower

One of the area's most interesting observation towers. The historic iron structure on the summit of Studenec offers views across Bohemian Switzerland and the Lusatian Mountains.

6.8 km approx. 2 h 30 min on foot
FIND OUT MORE →
WALKING & CYCLING · ALL YEAR

Chřibská Reservoir

438 m above sea level · reservoir

A peaceful spot hidden in the forests above Chřibská. The reservoir on the Chřibská Kamenice river makes a pleasant destination for a shorter walk from LENA.

4.8 km approx. 1 h 30 min on foot
Find out more →
WALKING & CYCLING · ALL YEAR

Doubice

picturesque village · Bohemian Switzerland

A picturesque village between the Lusatian Mountains and Bohemian Switzerland. Visit its well-known outdoor woodcarving gallery and enjoy the relaxed atmosphere of one of the smallest villages in Czechia.

5.9 km approx. 26 min by bike
Find out more →
WALKING & CYCLING · ALL YEAR

Panská skála

578 m above sea level · basalt columns

One of the region's best-known sights. Striking basalt columns resembling organ pipes form a unique rock formation, also featured in the Czech fairy-tale film The Proud Princess.

16.9 km approx. 19 min by car
Find out more →
BOHEMIAN SWITZERLAND

Rocks, viewpoints and places with a story.

Eight ideas for exploring Bohemian Switzerland. From famous viewpoints to places hidden deep in the forests.

WALKING & CYCLING · ALL YEAR

Mariina vyhlídka

428 m above sea level · rocky viewpoint

One of Bohemian Switzerland's iconic viewpoints. This rocky summit above Jetřichovice offers beautiful views across a landscape of sandstone cliffs.

9.2 kmapprox. 47 min by bike
Find out more →
WALKING & CYCLING · ALL YEAR

Dolský mlýn

historic ruins · Bohemian Switzerland

The romantic ruins of a historic mill hidden in the deep Kamenice valley. One of Bohemian Switzerland's most atmospheric places, accessible on foot or by bike.

11.4 kmapprox. 44 min by bike
Find out more →
WALKING & CYCLING · ALL YEAR

Falkenštejn castle ruins

303 m above sea level · rock castle

The remains of a medieval rock castle above Jetřichovice. Iron stairs now lead to the top and a viewing platform with beautiful views across Bohemian Switzerland.

8.1 kmapprox. 36 min by bike
Find out more →
WALKING & CYCLING · ALL YEAR

Kyjov Reservoir

natural swimming · Kyjov

Natural swimming surrounded by forest. A pleasant destination for a summer day, which you can combine with a walk or bike ride from LENA via Doubice.

8 kmapprox. 33 min by bike
Find out more →
WALKING · ALL YEAR

Rudolfův kámen

484 m above sea level · rocky viewpoint

A striking sandstone rock above Jetřichovice, topped by a wooden viewing pavilion. Steps and ladders lead up to rewarding views of Bohemian Switzerland.

11 kmapprox. 3 h 38 min on foot
Find out more →
WALKING & CYCLING · ALL YEAR

Jetřichovice

234 m above sea level · the heart of Bohemian Switzerland

A picturesque village in the heart of Bohemian Switzerland and a natural starting point for walks among sandstone rocks and viewpoints. An ideal base for exploring the area's best-known sights.

7.5 kmapprox. 29 min by bike
Find out more →
WALKING · ALL YEAR

Růžovský vrch

620 m above sea level · the highest peak in Bohemian Switzerland

A distinctive basalt landmark, also nicknamed Děčín's Mount Fuji. The highest peak in Bohemian Switzerland offers a beautiful walk through a national nature reserve.

21.4 kmapprox. 28 min by car
Find out more →
WALKING & CYCLING · ALL YEAR

Trpasličí skála

319 m above sea level · rock formation

A rocky trail near Jetřichovice with small dwarf figures carved into the sandstone. A short walk with a fairy-tale feel, especially enjoyable for families with children.

5.8 kmapprox. 24 min by bike
Find out more →
WINTER

When the snow arrives, just choose a direction.

Skiing, cross-country trails or simply a walk through a winter landscape. Near LENA you'll find small family ski areas and cross-country routes through Bohemian Switzerland and the Lusatian Mountains.

DOWNHILL SKIING

Horní Podluží

A small ski area for a relaxed day on the slopes. A good choice for families with children, or whenever you don't want to travel far to ski.

7.2 kmapprox. 10 min by car

DOWNHILL & CROSS-COUNTRY SKIING

Polevsko

A family ski area with a friendly atmosphere. Nearby, the Lusatian ski trail network also offers routes for cross-country skiing.

13.5 kmapprox. 20 min by car

Around Luž

LUSATIAN SKI TRAIL NETWORK

Miles of winter trails

From Polevsko through Nová Huť, Šébr and Myslivny to Luž. The Lusatian ski trail network offers around 100–130 km of connected and adjoining trails through the Lusatian Mountains. Ideal for both recreational skiers and experienced winter enthusiasts.
