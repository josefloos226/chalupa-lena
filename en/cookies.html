Cookies and website analytics

Our website uses essential functions and optional Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-7X0S3GPS47) to understand overall visitor numbers, how people use the site and how many enquiries are successfully submitted. Google Analytics is enabled only if you choose “Allow analytics”. We never send the content of your enquiry form to Analytics.

You can change your choice or withdraw your consent at any time using “Cookie settings” below. We store your preference in your browser (local storage). Google Analytics may set cookies (such as _ga) after you allow analytics. For information about Google’s data processing, visit Google’s privacy policy.

If you use the embedded Google Map, Google may process data as a separate provider. For privacy enquiries, contact us at info@chalupa-lena.cz.
