Staying at LENA

A house to call your own for a while.

During your stay, LENA is entirely yours. We do not rent individual rooms, so you won't share the cottage with other guests. It's your space for time together, peaceful moments and your own pace.

Fully renovated, the cottage retains its original character while offering modern comfort. It stands in a quiet part of Dolní Chřibská, surrounded by a garden and the landscapes of Bohemian Switzerland and the Lusatian Mountains.

COTTAGE RENTAL IN DOLNÍ CHŘIBSKÁ

Your own cottage. Two beautiful regions to explore.

Looking for an entire holiday cottage near Bohemian Switzerland for family or friends? LENA is set in a quiet part of Dolní Chřibská, between Bohemian Switzerland and the Lusatian Mountains. The whole cottage is yours during your stay, with four bedrooms, space for up to 10 guests, two bathrooms and a generous shared living area.

Outside, there is a garden with seating, a fire pit and space for children to play. Spend the day exploring sandstone landscapes, viewpoints or cycling routes, then return to the calm of the cottage.
