Before your stay at Chalupa LENA

In Dolní Chřibská, you rent the entire cottage, not individual rooms. Four separate bedrooms accommodate up to 10 guests. The cottage has two bathrooms with toilets, an equipped kitchen, a garden and four parking spaces on site. Below you will find the details on arrival, facilities and house rules.
